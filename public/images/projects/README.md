- `total-wash-project.jpg` — Completed Total Wash automated vehicle washing facility (Metro Cash & Carry / Total Service Center)
- `total-quartz-retail-interior.jpg` — Total / Total Quartz retail shop interior (Total 2020 Shop standard)
- `defense-road-filling-station.jpg` — Shell Defense Road Filling Station forecourt, canopy & building
- `markhoor-filling-station.jpg` — Total Parco Markhoor Filling Station complete greenfield construction
- `nti-karal-cng-construction.jpg` — Shell NTI Karal CNG Station civil & high-pressure compressor works
- `machikay-depot-rectification.jpg` — Total Parco Machikay Bulk Petroleum Depot T-1 civil defect rectification
- `canopy-rectification.jpg` — Multi-site canopy structural reinforcement & ACP fascia replacement
- `oil-water-separator-total.jpg` — Multi-chamber reinforced concrete oil-water separator installations

Supported extensions: `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp`, `.svg`.
Image handling is fully static-export compatible with `unoptimized: true` in `next.config.ts`.
